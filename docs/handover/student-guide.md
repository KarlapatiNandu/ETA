# Bus Mitra — student guide

For students, shared at launch (a poster QR code, the department WhatsApp group). Short on
purpose.

---

## Get started (2 minutes)

1. Open **busmitra.in** on your phone.
2. **Claim your account**: enter your roll number. A code comes by SMS to the phone number the
   Transport Department has for you. Enter it and choose a password.
   *No SMS?* Your number may be missing or wrong in the college records — ask the Transport
   Department to update it.
3. **Install it** (recommended): Android — *Add to Home screen* when asked. iPhone — Safari →
   Share → **Add to Home Screen**. On iPhone this is the **only** way to get alerts on the phone;
   until you do, urgent alerts come by SMS.
4. **Settings** → **Turn on alerts**, then set **Home** (drag the pin — it is stored to about
   100 m, never your exact door) and how you get to the stop (walk, cycle, bike, car).
5. **Star** your bus, and mark one as **my bus**.

## Every day

- The big card says **when your bus reaches your stop** and **when to leave home**. The range
  ("6–8 min") is honest: a wider range means less certainty.
- **Amber — "last seen 40 s ago"**: the bus missed a few updates. **Red — no signal**: it is
  quiet; the app shows exactly when it was last seen and never guesses where it is now. In a
  **known dead zone** it tells you how long it usually lasts.
- **On the bus?** Tap **I'm on the bus** to silence alerts for the rest of the trip.

## Alerts you can get

| | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Your bus is not coming | Push + SMS, even in quiet hours |
| **Important** | Your bus started; leave now; signal lost | Push (SMS if push is off) |
| **Normal** | Stop-by-stop progress of a starred bus | Push, collapsed into one updating alert |
| **Low** | Known dead zones | In the app only |

Everything also lands in the **bell** in the app, even if your phone was off. **Settings** has
quiet hours, a per-bus mute and the level of alerts you want.

## Privacy

Your location is used only on your phone to show the map, and your home pin is stored rounded to
~100 m. Admins can see how many students got an announcement, never who.
