# Bus Mitra — driver sheet

One page, printed and kept in the bus. For drivers of buses that carry a **phone** as the
tracker. Buses with a wired tracker box need nothing from the driver at all.

> ⚠️ The Telugu and Hindi lines below are a first draft and **must be checked by a native
> speaker at the Transport Department before this sheet is printed.**

---

## What the screen tells you

- **GPS ±8 m** (green) — all good. **GPS: waiting** — go on driving; it finds the sky in a minute.
- **N pings sent · M waiting to send** — "waiting" grows in a dead zone and empties by itself
  when the signal returns. **Nothing is lost**, and you do not need to do anything.
- **Offline** — the same: keep driving, keep the screen open.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| The app asks to **pair this phone** | Call the Transport Department. They send a new pairing link. Do not open links from anyone else. |
| **"This phone is not assigned to a bus yet"** | Call the Transport Department. |
| You forgot to press START | Press it now. Students see the bus from that moment. |
| You forgot to press END | Press it as soon as you remember. The bus leaves the students' map by itself after 10 minutes without signal. |
| Phone battery died | Charge it, open the app — the trip continues where it stopped. |
| The phone is lost or stolen | Tell the Transport Department **the same day**: they switch the phone off from the console so nobody can pretend to be your bus. |

## What Bus Mitra records — and what it does not

- It records **where the bus is while a trip is running**. It stops the moment you press END.
- It does **not** track you: nothing is recorded outside a trip, and your name is not attached to
  the positions. Positions are kept 90 days and then only averages (how long each road takes) are
  kept.
